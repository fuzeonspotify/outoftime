# Realistic Car Audio

The Memory Road Porsche uses a quiet layered vehicle sound system built from the following CC0 Freesound recordings:

## Engine loop

- **Sound:** Sedan engine loop
- **Creator:** Dmitry_mansurev64
- **Freesound ID:** `748027`
- **Source:** https://freesound.org/people/Dmitry_mansurev64/sounds/748027/
- **License:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- **Published format:** Ogg Vorbis, 48 kHz, stereo, 192 kbps

## Interior road bed

- **Sound:** Inside Car Noise While Driving
- **Creator:** jackthemurray
- **Freesound ID:** `439311`
- **Source:** https://freesound.org/people/jackthemurray/sounds/439311/
- **License:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- **Published source format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo

The installer retrieves Freesound's high-quality Ogg previews for consistent Godot runtime playback. The recordings are spatially layered, crossfaded, pitch-adjusted from actual vehicle speed, and intentionally mixed at a restrained volume.
